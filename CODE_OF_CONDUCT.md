# 行为准则 / Code of Conduct

## 中文

参与本项目（包括 issue、pull request、讨论和其他社区空间）即表示你同意：

- 尊重其他参与者，不进行骚扰、歧视、威胁、人肉搜索或恶意攻击。
- 不发布真实凭据、私人账号数据、抓包内容或其他敏感信息。
- 不提交或推广绕过订阅、额度、验证码、速率限制或访问控制的功能。
- 不利用本项目进行账号滥用、凭据窃取、批量注册或其他违法活动。
- 尊重维护者的时间，先搜索已有 issue，并提供可复现、已脱敏的信息。

违反上述准则的内容可能被编辑、隐藏或删除，相关参与者可能被限制参与。涉及安全漏洞或敏感数据时，请勿公开争执，改用 [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md) 中的私密报告方式。

维护者不提供法律、合规或账号安全建议。完整使用风险见 [DISCLAIMER.md](./DISCLAIMER.md)。

## English

By participating in this project (including issues, pull requests, discussions and other community spaces), you agree to:

- Be respectful and refrain from harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing or personal attacks.
- Never post real credentials, private account data, packet captures or other sensitive information.
- Never submit or promote features that bypass subscriptions, quotas, captchas, rate limits or access controls.
- Never use this project for account abuse, credential theft, bulk registration or other unlawful activity.
- Respect maintainer time: search existing issues first and provide reproducible, redacted information.

Content that violates these expectations may be edited, hidden or removed, and participants may be restricted. For security vulnerabilities or sensitive data, do not argue in public; use the private reporting method in [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md).

The maintainers do not provide legal, compliance or account-security advice. See [DISCLAIMER.md](./DISCLAIMER.md) for the full risk notice.
